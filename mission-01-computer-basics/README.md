# Mission 1 - Know Your Computer

## 1. Task / objective
Explain how a computer receives input, processes information, produces output and stores results, and identify hardware, software and everyday computer-based systems.

## 2. Work completed
- Traced one activity (listening to music) through the IPOS stages.
- Listed hardware and software items with their types and purposes.
- Described three everyday computer-based systems.

### Task 1.1 - IPOS: Listening to music

| Stage | What happens in my example | Hardware / component |
|---|---|---|
| Input | I click Play on the trackpad. | Trackpad |
| Processing | The CPU processes the audio while RAM holds the data being used. | CPU, RAM |
| Output | The speakers play the music. | Speakers |
| Storage | A downloaded song is saved on the SSD; a streamed song may come from cloud storage. | SSD, cloud storage |

When I click Play on the trackpad, the CPU processes the audio and the RAM holds the data being used. The sound comes out through the speakers. The song is saved on the SSD (or comes from cloud storage if I stream it).

## 4. My understanding
IPOS means Input, Processing, Output and Storage. Hardware is the physical part of a computer, and software gives it instructions. They work together to perform tasks, such as playing music or saving a file. Understanding IPOS helps me see how a computer handles information.
